About Quantum Lite

BCA 3rd Semester Minor Project — Jaypee Institute of Information Technology (JIIT)

Project Overview & Objectives

Quantum Lite bridges the gap between abstract linear-algebraic quantum mechanics and hands-on experimentation. Built for beginners learning quantum computing, it lets students design circuits visually while delegating all complex matrix and state-vector calculations to a modular C++17 simulation engine.

  • Create, resize, and reset multi-qubit quantum circuits interactively.
  • Apply Hadamard, Pauli-X, Pauli-Y, Pauli-Z, and two-qubit CNOT gates.
  • Compute normalized complex probability amplitudes using C++ OOP.
  • Visualize measurement probabilities and step-by-step gate transformations.
